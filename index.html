HOW WE WORK

A clear path from first call to handover.

No jargon, no pressure. Just a structured process you can follow at every stage.

01

Consultation

We start by understanding your budget, purpose and timeline, whether it is investment, a personal home or commercial use.

02

Property Matching

Our team shortlists verified options that fit your criteria, complete with pricing history and location context.

03

Site Visit & Due Diligence

We arrange physical or virtual site visits and independently verify ownership documents and society approvals.

04

Negotiation & Agreement

We negotiate on your behalf and draft a clear agreement with transparent terms before any payment changes hands.

05

Transfer & Handover

We manage registry transfer and paperwork, then hand over possession with full documentation in your name.
